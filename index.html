01 / INSTALL

Start with a safe draft.

Add Shelf as an unpublished theme first. This lets you set it up and preview it without changing your live store.

Shopify: adding and uploading themes ↗
1

In your Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes.

2

Click Add theme → Upload zip file, choose the Shelf zip file, and click Upload.

3

The theme appears in Theme library as unpublished. Click Customize to open the theme editor.

4

When you are happy with the result, click Publish. Your previous theme stays in your theme library.

Before you begin
Your products, collections, menus and pages are not part of the theme. They stay in your store when you change themes. Duplicate your current theme first if you want a backup.

02 / SECTIONS

Build your pages.

Every page of Shelf is made of sections. In the theme editor you can add, remove, hide and reorder them, and click a section to change its text, images and colors.

Signature product shelf (home page)

The interactive showroom. Visitors drag products from a shelf into a basket and see the cart update. Choose which products appear, set the basket color (red, black or blue), and add the product wave audio player if you want it.

Product page sections

The product page can include: product highlights, how-to steps, ingredients, a product video, before and after photos, related products, and complementary products. Each one can be added, hidden or reordered in the theme editor. Sections that have no content for a product hide themselves automatically.

Other sections

Announcement bar, image banner, category rail and gallery, collection spotlight, featured product, brand story and values, shoppable videos, video feature, shop the look, reviews marquee, FAQ, newsletter, trust bar, text ticker, and custom Liquid.

Theme settings

Colors, fonts, layout and cart options are in Theme settings (the gear icon in the theme editor). Changes there apply to the whole store.

03 / PRODUCT DATA

Fill the product fields.

Some product sections read their content from product fields (called metafields). You fill them once per product in Shopify admin → Products → (your product) → Metafields. The fields below are already created in the store where Shelf was set up. In a new store, create them in Settings → Custom data → Products using the namespace and key shown.

Writing the lists

  • Put one item per line.
  • To add a short description to an item, write it after a vertical bar: Shea butter | Softens and comforts dry skin
  • Items without a description are shown with the name only.

Ingredients — interactive points

In the Ingredients section, choose the style Interactive: product with tappable ingredient points. Visitors tap a numbered point on the product image to read about each ingredient.

  • By default the points are placed automatically.
  • To place a point yourself, add two numbers after the description: the position from the left and from the top, in percent. Example: Shea butter | Softens dry skin | 40 | 55
  • To use a different image than the product's first image, pick one in the section settings.
  • Choose Simple: photo cards or text list if you prefer a plain list.
  • Photos for ingredients: either fill Ingredient images on the product, or add Ingredient photo blocks to the section whose name matches the ingredient (this works for every product).

Before and after

The Before and after section compares two photos with a slider.

  • Section settings let you add comparisons manually (images or videos).
  • To show a different result on each product, fill Before image and After image on that product.
  • Turn on Product pages: only with own photos if you want the section hidden on products that have no photos of their own.
  • Use only real, unretouched photos. Results vary from person to person.

Product videos

Add a video to Product video, or upload videos to the product's media. On phones, several videos appear as a swipeable row where the centered video is larger.

04 / LAUNCH

Check before you publish.

Test your store as a customer would, on both a computer and a phone.

Shopify: placing a test order ↗
  • Add a product to the basket using the home page shelf, and again from a product page.
  • Change the quantity and check that the price updates.
  • Open the cart: change quantities, remove an item, and check the empty-cart message.
  • Continue to checkout and review shipping, taxes and discounts.
  • Place a test order with a test payment method, never a real card.
  • Check your menus, footer links and policy pages.
  • Switch the language if you use more than one, and check the right-to-left layout for Arabic.
  • Check keyboard navigation and text readability.

LANGUAGES

Languages.

FAQ

Good to know.

Can I publish Shelf immediately after uploading it?

It is better to keep it unpublished while you set it up and preview it. Shopify allows one published theme at a time, and your previous theme stays in your theme library.

Where do I change colors, fonts and cart behavior?

In the theme editor, open Theme settings (the gear icon). Section-specific options are in each section's settings.

Why does a product section not appear?

Sections that read product fields stay hidden when the product has no content for them. Fill the field on the product (see Product data), then reload the page.

Can I use my own photos in the Before and after section?

Yes. Fill the Before image and After image fields on the product, or add comparisons in the section settings.

What if I cannot find a setting mentioned here?

Open the section in the theme editor and look at its settings. If you still need help, go to the Support page.
