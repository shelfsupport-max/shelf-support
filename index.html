01 / INSTALL

Start with a safe draft.

Add Shelf as a draft theme first. That gives you room to review the storefront and customize it before making it live.

Shopify: adding and uploading themes ↗
1

In Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes.

2

Under Draft themes, choose Import theme → Upload zip file.

3

Select the Shelf ZIP and choose Upload. Keep the theme in draft while you review it.

Before you begin
Duplicate your current theme so you have a backup. Shopify themes are managed separately from products, collections, menus, and pages.

02 / SHAPE

Make the homepage yours.

The theme editor lets you preview changes while you work. Available sections, blocks, and settings depend on the theme and its version—use what appears in your Shelf editor.

▦

Homepage & shelf

Open the theme editor, choose the page or template you want to review, then add, remove, edit, or rearrange the sections your theme provides. Add your own products and collections through Shopify admin.

◌

Sections & settings

Use section and block controls for local content. Use Theme settings for store-wide options such as colors, typography, layout, and—if available—cart presentation. Save as you go.

↗

Preview before publish

Preview the draft theme on desktop and mobile, check navigation and product templates, and share a preview when you want feedback. Only publish once the whole path feels ready.

03 / LANGUAGES

Translate with a plan.

Shopify lets you add, publish, and manage store languages. Each published language gets its own URLs; where a translation is missing, Shopify displays your default-language content.

  1. Check the language requirements and whether your theme supports the setup you need.
  2. Prepare translations for products, pages, policies, checkout, and theme wording using Shopify’s supported tools.
  3. Preview the experience before publishing. Do not assume Shelf includes a particular language or translation until you see it in your theme and translation workflow.

FAQ

Good to know.

Can I publish Shelf immediately after uploading it?

It is better to keep it as a draft while you customize and preview it. Shopify allows only one published theme at a time; your previous theme remains available in Draft themes when you switch.

Where do I change colors, fonts, or cart behavior?

Open the theme editor and look under Theme settings. The controls shown are theme- and version-dependent. Sections and blocks can have their own individual settings.

Does Shelf include every Shopify feature?

No theme should be assumed to support every Shopify feature. Review the controls and templates exposed by your installed Shelf version, then confirm the result in preview and test mode.

What if I cannot find a setting mentioned here?

The setting may not be part of your theme version, may belong to a section rather than Theme settings, or may require a Shopify admin configuration. Contact support with the exact screen and step.
