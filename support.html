Prefer a public conversation?

For questions that do not include private information, open an issue in the Shelf support repository. A clear title and a short list of steps make it easier to understand.

Go to support issues ↗

Note: GitHub issues are public. Do not include passwords, access tokens, order details, payment data, private customer information or other sensitive data.

What to include in your message

  • Your store address (the .myshopify.com address).
  • The theme version (shown on the Guide page and in the theme library).
  • The page where the problem happens and what you expected to see.
  • The steps to repeat it.
  • A screenshot or short screen recording, if possible.
  • Your device and browser (for example: iPhone, Safari).

Support scope

Covered

  • Installing and updating the theme.
  • Using the theme's sections, blocks and settings.
  • Problems caused by the theme's code.
  • Questions about the product fields (metafields) that Shelf reads.

Not covered

  • Writing custom code or modifying the theme's files.
  • Third-party apps and their code.
  • Products, prices, shipping, taxes and payment settings in your store.
  • Content you add (photos, videos, audio, text) and the rights to use it.
